FAQ

Q. Do I need to create an account?

Not necessarily. We stick to an "open and use" principle — no sign-up, login, or account linking required. Download the app and you can start using its local management features right away; if you want cross-device sync or to share a household with family, you can sign in with an Apple or Google account to enable that.

Q. Where does my data get uploaded to?

Beri is built local-first. If you don't sign in, your inventory, spaces, photos, and purchase records are stored primarily on your device, and can sync to your personal iCloud based on your iCloud settings; we don't keep a separate server copy of that data. If you choose to sign in with an Apple or Google account, the app instead creates an account and syncs the data above through Google's cloud service (Firebase), for cross-device use and household sharing (see our Privacy Policy for details). We only receive the contact and issue details you choose to provide when you submit feedback.

Q. How do I share with my family?

You can choose to "invite someone to co-manage this household," letting family members jointly manage the same home; or use "share just this space" or "ask someone to help shop," which shares a read-only copy of specific content. A read-only copy is best for reference or shopping — later changes aren't guaranteed to sync automatically.

Beri, your home's restocking brain.

Remembers what's in your home, where it's stored, and when to buy more.
From tracking stock to shopping, Beri keeps tabs on the everyday essentials you always forget about until you run out.

Download on the App Store See the app

Standing in the store, drawing a blank?

Jot down what needs restocking in real time and track unit prices and price history. Quickly confirm what you need at the store and make the smartest purchase decision.

Only notice it's expired while tidying up?

Set custom low-stock thresholds and expiry reminders. Get notified before easy-to-forget essentials, consumables, or food expire — cut down on waste and keep your household running smoothly.

Bought it, but can't remember where you put it?

Assign items to zones around your home — kitchen, fridge, storage room, and more. Quickly find where things are and run a space-by-space stock check, so everything has its place.

Want family to help manage things or shop?

Invite family members to co-manage the whole household, or share a read-only copy of just one space or a shopping list. Share exactly the scope you need — no need to expose your whole inventory.

Local-first. Your data belongs to you.

We believe your household's everyday privacy deserves real protection. Beri is designed local-first — you can use it without signing in: your inventory, spaces, photos, and purchase records are stored primarily on your Apple device, and if you turn on iCloud sync, they're securely stored in your personal private cloud via Apple iCloud. If you choose to sign in with an Apple or Google account, Beri instead syncs your data through Google's cloud service (Firebase), so you can use it across devices and share a household with family members. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

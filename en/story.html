Our Story

Free up headspace,
for what matters more.

Your supplies, sorted by Beri.

Does this sound familiar at your house?

"Honey, where's the <whatever>?"

"Dad, are we running low on <whatever>?"

"Is this 'buy one get one' deal actually cheaper?"

"How much did we pay for diapers last time?"

These all look like small, everyday questions.

But what's actually exhausting isn't answering them — it's having to keep remembering them in the first place.

Remembering how much shampoo is left.
Remembering whether you're almost out of toilet paper.
Remembering which brand of formula to buy.
Remembering which store has it cheaper.
Remembering which cabinet it's in.

Bit by bit, day by day, these invisible little things slowly fill up your mind.

It wasn't until I became a mother that I realized: what wears you down isn't the housework — it's constantly having to remember.

Finally getting the kids to sleep, then staying up to compare prices.
Standing in the store, trying hard to recall how much is left at home.
Seeing a promotion and suddenly doing mental math on discounts, sizes, shipping, and unit prices.

Sometimes buying a pack of diapers feels like taking a math test.

I tried spreadsheets, notes apps, paper lists — and all kinds of apps.

They could record data. But they couldn't actually lighten the mental load that everyday life brings.

I kept thinking: what if there were a tool that didn't just manage things, but actually helped carry the weight of remembering all those little things? How great would that be.

And so, Beri was born.

Beri is a little spirit that lives in your home.

It loves tidying up, and it's great at remembering the small things that are easy to forget.

It keeps track of your household supplies with you, organizes every cabinet, tracks inventory, helps compare prices — and can share it all with your family too.

It's not just about making life more efficient.

It's about this: when someone asks,

"Do we still have any at home?"

you won't have to rack your brain.

When you walk into a store, you won't have to do mental math.

When your family needs a hand, everyone already knows what to do.

We've always believed —

what's truly worth remembering shouldn't be how many rolls of toilet paper are left, where the shampoo is, or how much the diapers cost last time.

Those things can be handed off to Beri.

Because your mind isn't meant to hold onto every little chore.

It should be saved for your kid's discovery today, a family's laughter, a trip, a dinner together — the memories that truly matter.
